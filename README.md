# 🍯 ApiVera

### AI • IoT • Blockchain Powered Honey Intelligence & Traceability Platform

**ApiVera** is the application layer of **HoneyChain**, designed to create a trusted digital ecosystem for beekeepers, honey producers, and consumers.

ApiVera connects **Smart Hives, AI-powered insights, honey quality data, blockchain-based traceability, and QR verification** into one unified platform.

> **From Hive to Honey — Every Drop Has a Story.**

---

## 🚀 What is ApiVera?

The honey industry faces several challenges:

* Counterfeit and adulterated honey
* Lack of transparent honey origin
* Limited monitoring of hive health
* Manual beekeeping management
* Poor access to real-time hive insights
* Lack of trustworthy information for consumers
* Fragmented honey supply chains

ApiVera addresses these problems by connecting the complete honey lifecycle:

```text
🐝 Hive
   ↓
📡 IoT Monitoring
   ↓
🤖 AI Analysis
   ↓
🍯 Harvest
   ↓
🧪 Quality Verification
   ↓
🔗 Blockchain Traceability
   ↓
📱 QR Honey Passport
   ↓
👤 Consumer Verification
```

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 🐝 Smart Hive Monitoring

Monitor connected hives using IoT sensors.

Track:

* Temperature
* Humidity
* Hive weight
* Activity patterns
* Environmental conditions
* Hive health indicators

---

## 🤖 AI-Powered Hive Intelligence

ApiVera analyzes hive data and identifies unusual patterns.

Examples:

* Abnormal temperature
* Sudden weight changes
* Reduced hive activity
* Environmental anomalies
* Potential colony health risks

AI converts raw sensor data into understandable insights and recommendations.

---

## 🍯 Honey Batch Management

Every harvest can be registered as a unique digital honey batch.

Each batch can contain:

* Batch ID
* Hive ID
* Beekeeper
* Harvest date
* Honey variety
* Origin
* Quantity
* Processing information
* Quality information

---

## 🔗 Blockchain Traceability

Important verification events are recorded using blockchain technology.

This creates a tamper-evident history for honey batches.

```text
Hive → Harvest → Quality → Processing → Product
```

Consumers can verify the authenticity and history of a product without needing to understand blockchain technology.

---

## 📱 QR Honey Passport

Every verified honey product can have a unique QR code.

A consumer scans the QR code and can view:

```text
🍯 Honey Passport

✓ Source Verified
✓ Harvest Verified
✓ Quality Verified
✓ Processing Recorded
✓ Blockchain Verified
```

The goal is simple:

> **Scan → Verify → Trust**

---

## 🚨 Counterfeit Detection

ApiVera can monitor unusual QR verification activity.

For example, if the same product identity is repeatedly scanned from unexpected locations, the system can flag the product for investigation.

```text
⚠️ Suspicious Activity

Batch: AV-2026-00192

Risk Level: HIGH

Unusual verification activity detected.
```
